## Abstract

As Artificial Intelligence reaches unprecedented levels of generative capability, the boundaries between historical fact and synthetic reality are rapidly dissolving. The internet is facing an entropy of truth. **Project Pure Land** is an open-source initiative dedicated to preserving a strictly AI-free sanctuary of human history and contemporary milestones. Through a combination of physical isolation, cryptographic immutability, and an oligarchical human-consensus governance model, we aim to build the ultimate reference frame for objective reality.

---

## 1. The Problem: The Entropy of Truth

The marginal cost of fabricating hyper-realistic historical documents, images, and events has dropped to near zero. Malicious actors, leveraging generative AI, can dynamically rewrite history to manipulate public perception, leading to the "Liar's Dividend"—a state of zero-trust where everything is dismissed as fake. When history becomes fluid, human society loses its foundation for consensus.

---

## 2. Core Architecture

Project Pure Land is not a standard web application; it is a fortified informational vault built on three architectural pillars:

*   **The Genesis Node:** The primary server cluster is physically located in Auckland, New Zealand, chosen for its geopolitical stability and data sovereignty laws. Mirrored cold-storage backups will be distributed globally.
*   **Absolute Read-Only Public Access:** The database is completely air-gapped from public write permissions. It operates as an informational lighthouse. There are no APIs for public content submission, no comment sections, and no dynamic content rendering.
*   **Git-Blockchain Hybrid Ledger:** All editorial actions utilize a Git-based Merkle tree structure, requiring multi-signature GPG authentication from the Council. Repository snapshots are periodically broadcasted to a Layer-1 blockchain (e.g., Ethereum) to mathematically prove cryptographic immutability.

---

## 3. Governance: The 12×12 Epistemic Council

Truth cannot be crowdsourced in an age of AI pollution. Project Pure Land relies on an oligarchical trust network:

*   **The Council of 12:** The project is governed by 12 authoritative figures from the humanities and sciences with impeccable public credibility.
*   **The Sub-Councils:** Each of the 12 Council members leads a dedicated team of 12 domain experts (historians, journalists, forensic data analysts).
*   **The Squads:** These teams are further divided into 3-4 specialized squads responsible for vetting specific events.
*   **Consensus Mechanism:** A proposed entry becomes "Immutable History" only after achieving a supermajority cryptographic signature from the Council. Any modification to existing entries requires a transparent, permanently logged consensus vote.

---

## 4. The Zero-AI Protocol

Submissions must pass the Zero-AI verification pipeline.

*   **Prohibited:** Use of LLMs for drafting, AI translation, generative image models (e.g., Midjourney, DALL-E), and AI-based audio/video restoration.
*   **Permitted:** Basic computational aids (spell checkers, hardware-level ISP noise reduction).
*   **Chain of Custody:** Original hardware signatures (e.g., C2PA metadata) must remain intact from the point of capture to Council review.
